Christmas cake and pudding schedule

Pick the day you bake and get a dated list: feeding days, the last feed, marzipan, icing and steaming the pudding on Christmas Day. Stir-up Sunday (the last Sunday before Advent) is filled in for you. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Rules used: feed with 1 to 2 tablespoons of brandy, rum, sherry or fruit juice; stop feeding the cake 6 days before marzipan (our suggestion, so the top dries); marzipan 2 days before icing (our suggestion); stop feeding the pudding on 18 December (our suggestion); steam the pudding at least 2 hours on Christmas Day until it is 70C in the centre. These are sensible defaults, not a recipe: follow your own recipe where it differs. Alcohol-free puddings have no spirit to preserve them, so use within about four to six weeks or freeze.
